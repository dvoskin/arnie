## ⭐⭐⭐ The lesson that cost the day

**v1 recorded the tree SHA and a self-tested reader. Both were true. Neither
pinned the product.** All 18 flags `render.yaml` declares were unset:
`FOOD_GATE_MODEL` unset admitted 2 of 25 cases to the structured lane instead
of 25 of 25; `NUTRITION_RESOLVER_MODE` was off against a declared `live`;
`DEFAULT_MODEL` was **a different model**.

Fixed at `58e6413`: the harness parses `render.yaml`, refuses to run on any
undeclared flag mismatch, and writes the resolved config as the output's first
line. A deviation is legal only with a written reason in `_ALLOWED_DEVIATIONS`.

**Two published conclusions were reversed by this**: DEFAULTABILITY (closed at
population 1 → open at 8) and representation instability (3 cases → 0). One
registration was rewritten (`CF28`) and one deploy was held (`27983be`).
